# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version.
- Bento home with a highlighted headline, the newest post, the pile of coloured book spines, a newsletter tile, category chips and post cards.
- Single post with a colour-card header, contents chips, pull quote and previous/next tiles.
- Archive with a count pill, category chips, row cards and a pill pager; category, author, search and plain page layouts.
- A 404 with a toppled pile.
- Dark mode: follows the system with `auto`, and the header toggle switches it.
- Settings: `home_headline`, `recent_style`, `show_newsletter`, `corners` and `archive_path`, next to `color_mode` and `density`.
