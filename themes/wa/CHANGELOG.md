# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release: a magazine blog theme with a mosaic hero of notched image cards, numbered picks, a ticker, topic chips, a night band and a reading queue.
- Layouts for home, post, page, archive, category and tag, author, search and 404.
- Light and dark modes with a header toggle; colours, fonts and corners follow Theme > Colours and fonts.
- Settings for colour mode, spacing, section toggles, headings, announcement, newsletter and signal colour.
- Instrument Sans and Fraunces italic, self-hosted.
