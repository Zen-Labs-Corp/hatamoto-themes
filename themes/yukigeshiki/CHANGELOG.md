# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First version.
- Layouts: home, single post, archive, category and tag, author, search and 404; plain pages restyled.
- A sticky sidebar with a split masthead and the category list, a chip row on small screens, and a two-column card grid on category tints.
- Settings: colour mode (default follows the system), spacing, split masthead, tinted cards, show author, header button label and address, footer quote, archive address.
- Light and dark colour modes, with a toggle and a saved choice; text meets WCAG AA contrast in both.
- Bricolage Grotesque and Source Serif 4, self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
