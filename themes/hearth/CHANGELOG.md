# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Comments: the comment form and thread on posts and pages now use Hearth's own type, colours and
  underlined fields instead of the core's default look. They show only when comments are on.

## 1.0.0

- Figtree type, a paper and terracotta palette, light and dark modes.
- Sticky header with an outlined button and a menu that works without JavaScript.
- Split hero with an optional house outline, dark features band, hairline process and FAQ rows, photo call-to-action band.
- Split sections with a full-bleed dark panel, styled content, pricing, team and contact sections.
- Blog archive, post, search and 404 pages in the same style.
- Terracotta footer band with grain, headline, contact details and a back-to-top control.
- Nine settings for the header button, footer text and decorations.
