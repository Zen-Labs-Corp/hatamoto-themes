# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release: a dense magazine blog theme in indigo and saffron.
- Layouts for home, single post, archive, category and tag, author, search and 404.
- Lead story, three-column grid, numbered sidebar with topic chips and an optional subscribe band.
- Ten settings: colour mode, spacing, issue strip and label, home page layout, sidebar and its title, subscribe link and headline, accent.
- Three accent presets (saffron, brick, teal) and a dark mode, both checked for AA contrast.
- Self-hosted Instrument Sans and Source Serif 4.
