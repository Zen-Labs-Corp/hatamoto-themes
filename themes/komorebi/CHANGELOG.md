# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release: a quiet, typographic blog theme in light and dark, indigo and peach.
- Layouts for the home page, single posts, pages, the archive, categories and tags, authors, search and the 404 page.
- Home page with a two-tone split hero, a featured card beside recent posts, a topics grid and an about band.
- Generated pattern art for posts and topics without a featured image.
- Settings for the colour mode, spacing, the hero lines, the header button and footer line (the site's own header button wins; no address hides it), the post archive address, the about statement, the home page blocks, the topics grid and the generated art.
- Header submenus open on hover and focus on desktop; the dark palette keeps your primary colour.
- Inter Tight and IBM Plex Mono are bundled (SIL Open Font License).
